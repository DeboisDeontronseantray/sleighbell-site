Sleighbell Line

A phone call from Santa, booked by a parent or guardian.

How it works

  1. An adult calls the Sleighbell Line at (254) 875-0094 to book a Santa call.
  2. Pick a day and time. Santa calls at the time you booked.
  3. Optional: get a text with your booking confirmation and a reminder. Texts are optional and not required to use the service.

Booking texts

If the adult booking says yes on the call, we send up to 3 texts per booking (confirmation and reminder). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. No marketing. See how SMS opt-in works.
