SMS Opt-In: Sleighbell Line booking texts

How you opt in

You opt in verbally during a phone call to the Sleighbell Line at (254) 875-0094. Consent is asked only of the adult booking the call. Near the end of the call the automated assistant reads this script, word for word:

"Would you like a text from the Sleighbell Line at this number with your booking confirmation and call reminder? It's up to 3 messages. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP anytime to opt out, HELP for help. Texts are optional."

Only a clear yes is recorded. Each consent is logged with the call ID, time and script version.

  • Texts are optional. Texting is not required to book or use the service, and consent is never a condition of purchase.
  • Frequency: up to 3 messages per booking.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • STOP: reply STOP at any time to opt out. You get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • HELP: reply HELP, or email help@sleighbellcalls.com.

Sample messages

  • "Sleighbell Line: Your Santa call is booked for [date] at [time] CT. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "Sleighbell Line: Reminder, Santa calls tomorrow at [time] CT. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
  • Opt-in confirmation: "Sleighbell Line: You're subscribed to booking texts, up to 3 per booking. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
